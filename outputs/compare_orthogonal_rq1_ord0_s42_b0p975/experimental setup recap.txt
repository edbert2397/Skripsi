\subsection{Experimental Setup Recap}
\label{subsec:setupRecap}

All methods are evaluated under the same training configuration to
ensure that any difference in performance can be attributed to the
replay selection rule (or its absence, in the No Replay lower bound),
not to differences in optimisation or model capacity.

\paragraph*{Backbone and adaptation.} The shared backbone is
T5-Large with all pretrained weights frozen, adapted via Dual LoRA
(fast and slow adapters, rank $r=8$, $\alpha=32$) attached to the
$\mathbf{W}_Q$ and $\mathbf{W}_V$ projections of every attention block.
The slow adapter is updated through an exponential moving average of
the fast adapter with decay $\beta=0.995$ and is the adapter used at
evaluation time
(Section~\ref{subsec:dualLoraEMA}).

\paragraph*{Benchmark and budget.} The benchmark is the LNT 14-task
sequence in Order~0 with seed~42. Each task contributes
$2{,}000$ training samples and a held-out test split. With a
micro-batch size of~$8$ and gradient accumulation factor of~$8$, this
gives an effective batch size of~$64$ and roughly~$31$ optimiser
steps per task.

\paragraph*{Replay buffer.} The buffer follows the SuRe convention of
storing $2\%$ of the total training corpus, giving a total budget of
$S = 0.02 \times (14 \times 2{,}000) = 560$ samples. The budget is
divided equally across tasks already learned, so the per-task quota
shrinks as the sequence progresses (Equation~\ref{eq:perTaskQuota}).
Table~\ref{tab:bufferDynamics} shows this evolution at selected
checkpoints. By the end of training, each task is represented by
only $40$ samples in the buffer, but the total memory footprint stays
fixed at $560$ throughout.

\paragraph*{Training mix.} Each training step uses a mixed batch of
current-task samples and replay samples drawn from $\mathcal{M}$, with
a fixed replay ratio of [\textit{TODO: 1:2 / 1:8}] across all replay
methods. Because the ratio, buffer size, and EMA decay are identical
for every replay method, the only variable is the scoring rule that
decides which samples enter $\mathcal{M}$.

\paragraph*{No Replay lower bound.} As a naive baseline we additionally
include a No Replay configuration that trains on the LNT sequence
without any buffer or replay step. The buffer remains empty throughout
($|\mathcal{M}|=0$ at every step) and each task is fitted purely from
its own $2{,}000$ training samples. All other components -- T5-Large
backbone, Dual LoRA adapters, EMA decay $\beta$, optimiser, and
schedule -- match the replay methods exactly. This isolates the
contribution of replay itself from the contribution of any particular
selection rule.
